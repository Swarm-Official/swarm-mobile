SWARM Wallet is a self-custody wallet for SwarmTestnet, an experimental proof-of-work network.

Create a wallet or restore one with a 24-word recovery phrase. Receive test SWM at a unified address, send test funds with an optional memo, and review your transaction history. The app supports device authentication and lets you choose a wallet server.

SWM test coins have no monetary value. The test network may reset, and a reset may erase balances. Keep your recovery phrase private. Anyone who has it can control your wallet, and the SWARM team cannot recover it for you.

Shielded transactions hide sender, receiver, and amount on the chain. Your wallet server can see your internet connection. The privacy features on this network have not had an independent security audit.

SWARM Wallet is open source and based on Zingo Mobile and zingolib. SWARM is independent of the Electric Coin Company, the Zcash Foundation, and Zingo Labs.
