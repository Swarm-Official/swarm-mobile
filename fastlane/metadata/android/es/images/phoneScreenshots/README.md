SWARM's screenshots are taken on the owner's phone and prepared with
`D:/privacy/scripts/play/letterbox_screenshots.py`, which pads each capture to
the aspect ratio Google Play accepts. Drop the prepared PNGs here, named
`1.png`, `2.png` and so on; Play wants between two and eight.
