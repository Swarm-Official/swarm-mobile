SWARM Wallet is the wallet for SWARM, a proof-of-work network with shielded transactions, currently running as a public TEST network (SwarmTestnet).

Read this first
• SWM test coins have no monetary value. Nobody sells them, and nobody should buy them.
• The test network can be reset at any time and every balance with it.
• No account, no email, no sign-up. The app creates a 24-word recovery phrase on your phone. Whoever holds those words controls the wallet. We never see them and cannot recover them.

What the app does
• Creates or restores a wallet from a 24-word recovery phrase.
• Receives SWM at a unified address; shielded by default.
• Sends SWM with an optional encrypted memo to the recipient.
• Shows your history, with mined rewards marked as such.
• Unlocks with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode; hides balances in "High privacy" mode.
• Lets you choose the wallet server, including one you run yourself.

Where the coins come from
Blocks are mined on ordinary computers with SWARM Node (a separate desktop program). Phones do not mine, and this app contains no miner. Every block pays 80 % to the miner and 20 % to three published project addresses; the rules are public and verified block by block.

Privacy, honestly
Shielded transactions hide sender, receiver and amount on the chain. They do not hide your internet address from the wallet server you connect to, and transparent addresses are public. The privacy code on this network is new and has not been independently audited. The app collects no data and contains no analytics; the privacy policy explains what the wallet server sees.
